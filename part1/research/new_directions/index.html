Part 1 new directions: completed experiments

No model from this work was promoted or submitted. All 136 scored settings across 14 studies are summarized here, including controls, diagnostic extensions and failed directions. They produced 926 complete out-of-fold repeats (4,630 outer folds) and 40 supplementary fits.

The summaries were checked against the saved predictions before removing bulky audit records. Code, settings, seeds, exact splits and environment versions remain available. These are sequential studies using reused development labels; negative results apply to the tested settings and comparators, not every possible use of a method. Early studies use weaker parents than the later C.1/LDA ensemble. This work did not test the newer anchored model.
