LDA research checkpoint

This branch’s LDA ensemble improved grouped CV to .931794 train / .921631 pooled, but scored .89401 on Kaggle, tying the previous ensemble. Fourteen later temporal and cutoff variants produced no confirmed ensemble upgrade. The separate anchored classifier subsequently scored a user-reported .89636; its local candidate and scored archive match the recorded hash.

This checkpoint documents completed comparisons, negative findings and failed versions. It does not retrain, upload, merge the newer model, or reinterpret old results against a new parent. CV and validation labels were repeatedly used during development. The original validation score does not validate the final235 refit.

Model and evaluation

The submitted ensemble uses 70% centered LDA-revision source, 15% classical tri-training and 15% SVM votes. The source combines .5 logistic regression (C=.1), .3 cosine neighbors and .2 shrinkage LDA, with two pseudo-label rounds and a native .3 cutoff. Fits are inductive by default; the direct-comparison target-input arm is explicitly separate. Gold labels remain unchanged, with held-group masked duplicates purged in grouped CV and chronology.

Saved grouped five-fold assignments use eight repeats in train157 and pooled235. Primary comparisons require positive means and at least five paired wins in both pools. Original validation78 and chronology98 are separate supplementary checks. Every learned step belongs to its fitting partition. No Part 2 data or external trial records were used.
