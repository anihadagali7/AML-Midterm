What was missing

The preserved 8a8c3fc Ani notebook changed the source model to .5 multinomial logistic regression, .3 cosine neighbors and .2 shrinkage LDA, and changed logistic C from .3 to .1. It also used that LDA mixture during pseudo-label teaching. The shared grouped benchmark had only evaluated the earlier source generation. This study adds an inductive adapter, with direct parity against the notebook's function definitions on identical permitted fitting data. Notebook input-loading, transductive fitting and export cells were not executed.

The notebook itself uses a different input-access regime and validation protocol. These measurements belong to this inductive adapter and cannot be assigned to its existing submission CSV. The highest reported Kaggle score remains .89423 for the older transductive artifact; the user reports another team near .93.

The new full ensemble also beats its C.1/no-LDA control by +.004777 train and +.003901 pooled. The separate paired repeat counts are in the contrast table; the gain is not solely the regularization change. Gold-only covariance heads and class-specific covariance models failed to improve the new revision consistently. A direct conditional sample-F1 utility expert, compared with matched independent-label regression, also failed the two-pool confirmation rule.

The utility experiment used the conditional expected-F1 decision principle described by Waegeman et al., implemented here as kernel regression of all 31 nonempty label-set utilities. Its finite-sample estimates have no Bayes-optimality guarantee. It used existing Part 1 labels and embeddings only.

Where the remaining gap is

Pooled Funding F1 improves from .769493 to .821196. Other precision rises from .707766 to .758236, but Other recall slips from .550000 to .539286. The strongest persistent Other misses are therefore still unresolved. The .931794 training-CV number must not be presented as .93 Kaggle performance, or as evidence that the leaderboard gap has been closed.
