PART 1 EXPERIMENT PROTOCOL — established before model fitting
Objective: sample-averaged multilabel F1. Report macro F1 and each label F1 as well.
External targets supplied by user: own Kaggle 0.89423; leader 0.92735. Neither is a local CV score.
Data permitted: supplied Part 1 CSVs and pathway_catalog.csv only. No external records, label recovery, pretrained/deep models, or ID-based features. Provided reason embeddings are allowed inputs.
Development: grouped multilabel-stratified 5-fold CV on the 157 visible training labels. Exact equal reason embeddings remain in one group. Exclude held-out labeled rows AND their exact-embedding duplicates from fold unsupervised fitting. Original masked rows supply the unlabeled pool. Training-label-only preprocessing, clustering label mapping, graph construction, calibration, feature selection, and pseudo-labeling.
Search: broad, structured model-family screen; refine promising settings; evaluate added feature blocks individually; test accepted combinations and removals. Compare standardization, geometry-preserving scaling, PCA, normalization, and covariance regularization. Record every attempted configuration, including failures, and save out-of-fold probabilities.
Models: linear/logistic/ridge, linear and kernel SVM, KNN, Gaussian NB, shrinkage discriminants, tree ensembles/boosting; k-means, mixtures, hierarchical, density and spectral clustering; graph propagation/spreading and weighted pseudo-label augmentation; conservative ensembles.
Decisions: use out-of-fold predictions to compare a small fixed threshold/expected-F1 decision family. Selection scores are development estimates, not unbiased generalization estimates. Audit selected candidates with fresh fold seeds, chronological holdouts, and nested CV with inner model/threshold selection.
Validation: do not examine validation labels/errors during broad search. Freeze a small shortlist and decision policies before one validation comparison, as the assignment permits model selection on part1_val.csv. Record all validation evaluations and never tune to specific validation errors. Preserve the first validation result. Do not claim a local validation score is a Kaggle score.
Chronology: assess early-to-later visible-label transfer; note rare-label enrichment confounds this stress test. Unlabeled later TRAIN rows may be used in SSL. Never fit on validation/test features during development, and never derive thresholds from their prevalence.
Final: retain true training labels; generate all 1,014 missing labels with an evaluated unsupervised/SSL procedure; create exact-format Kaggle submissions with ID preserved and five allowed label names. Default final fits use official training labels only; validation-refit must be separately identified if produced.
Reproducibility: fixed seeds, raw-data SHA256 hashes, locked package versions, CSV/JSON experiment log, saved predictions, figures and executable Jupyter notebooks. No claim of a guaranteed winning score.
