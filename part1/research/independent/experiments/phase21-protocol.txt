PHASE 21 — user-requested tri-training and target-aware model selection
Validation-plus-training refit is explicitly deferred by the user. The 78 validation labels are never model-fitting inputs. No test fitting or extra data. No deep methods.
1. Implement binary-relevance tri-training with three classical learners, bootstrap diversity, iterative cross-teaching and pair-agreement error/sample-size controls. Compare heterogeneous learners, labelwise confidence filters and small pseudo-label weights. Include a no-cross-teaching control. Any modifications to the original algorithm are documented and do not inherit its theoretical guarantees.
2. Estimate selection propensities using gold/masked status and only features from the 1,171 supplied training rows. Cross-fit the propensity model with identical-embedding groups. Use stabilized, capped density-ratio weights to rank out-of-fold sample-F1 toward the overall observed training population, with kernel mean matching as a sensitivity check. This is selection weighting; prior work weighted the training loss instead.
3. Freeze weights before inspecting reason-model rankings. Check effective sample size and sensitivity across weight estimators. This is an approximate covariate-shift correction: the gold subset was selected partly by reason label, so conditional invariance/overlap are not guaranteed.
4. Use existing grouped OOF predictions for an initial training-only screen. Refit a small diverse shortlist on three fresh grouped five-fold splits. Choose global decision policies only from those training predictions. Freeze at most five validation candidates: ordinary-CV tri-training winner, importance-weighted tri-training winner, target-weighted existing-model winner, robust-to-weight-estimator winner, and (if distinct) no-teaching control.
5. All external-validation predictions are saved before loading their labels for scoring. Record every score and do not hand-correct validation cases. Keep the incumbent unless an actual validation improvement occurs. Target remains validation sample-F1 > 0.92735; Kaggle score remains unknown.
