## Completed PySR experiment

The [bounded symbolic-classifier study](research/pysr_v1/README.md) completed all 84 standard cases, with 420 fresh symbolic heads. PySR alone scored .869559 / .884184 on Train157 / Pooled235, validation .844017 and chronology .750000. Adding 20% PySR to our submitted 75/25 blend also lowered all four headline checks. A linear head on the same geometry was included as a diagnostic; the reported 0.89935 anchored/Cand2 model was included as a matched control. The initial study ended without an export. The user subsequently requested the fixed 20% PySR blend as a manual trial; its [full235 preparation](research/pysr_blend_submission_v1/README.md) retains the exact CSV, model state, equations and predictions. It changes 9 of 312 labelsets from our 0.89850 submission. The user subsequently reported **0.89423**, a regression of **0.00427**, consistent in direction with the weaker local checks. The [exact-file result](submissions/results/current80-pysr20-trainval235.json) identifies its separate score tag; this candidate is not promoted. Independent local rescoring passed, and the nonwinning CV caches remain compact.

## Next experiment

The frozen testing policy and completed studies use anchored 0.89636 as their declared parent. Preserve those controls and rules, and include the submitted 0.89850 blend as a matched reference in future predeclared comparisons. Start with a small, explicit hypothesis and the saved grouped splits. Complete both pools and the supplementary checks before claiming a full comparison. A negative finding against an older parent does not establish that the same change fails against the new blend.

The [LDA study catalog](research/lda_ensemble/experiments/lda_research_checkpoint_v1/index.html) retains feature tests, failed versions, and selection rules. Its [temporal and decision follow-ups](research/lda_ensemble/experiments/temporal_generalization_review_v1/report.html) tested fourteen fixed variants without a confirmed ensemble upgrade. [New directions](research/new_directions/index.html) retains 136 settings across fourteen studies; none was promoted or submitted. Consult the relevant family before repeating an idea.

The [anchored transductive study](research/anchored_transductive_v1/README.md) completed all 84 standard cases for the unchanged parent and two target-input ablations. Geometry-only access slightly improved both CV means but failed the repeat-win rule and regressed on validation/chronology. Adding target-generated labels also failed the local advancement rule. Neither was promoted; the compact records include every repeat and supplementary result.

[CV and Kaggle have not always ranked our models alike](research/anchored_transductive_v1/README.md#cv-and-kaggle-ordering): LDA improved both CV means over the earlier ensemble but tied its Kaggle score; anchored was essentially tied with LDA on CV and improved Kaggle. Treat the transductive conclusion as insufficient local evidence to promote, not proof of a worse Kaggle result. Neither transductive variant has been submitted. The frozen evaluation and advancement rules remain unchanged.

The [three fixed neighbor/blend experiments](research/anchored_neighbor_blend_v1/README.md) also completed the full comparison. Collapsing duplicate neighbor groups and quarter-weighting generated neighbor votes hurt both CV means. The 75/25 blend improved Pooled235 from .921365 to .923404 with eight wins, but tied Train157's mean (3 wins / 3 ties / 2 losses), validation sample F1, and every chronological window. It did not pass the both-pool advancement rule. The study used 252 fresh scored fits plus matched saved LDA scores, not fresh LDA refits; full blend/control predictions remain retained.

The user subsequently authorized a manual Kaggle trial of that fixed blend and reported **0.89850** after receiving its exact CSV and scp command. Its [scored CSV and frozen preparation record](research/anchored_lda_blend_submission_v1/README.md) reuse the two verified full-235 fits. The +.00214 Kaggle change agrees in direction with the +.002039 pooled-CV change. This result supports the trial while leaving the original strict CV-rule outcome intact; it does not establish that CV and Kaggle rankings always agree. Uploaded bytes and leaderboard partition were not independently inspected.
