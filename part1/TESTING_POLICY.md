# Part 1 testing policy

The frozen grouped evaluation policy is preserved at
[research/lda_ensemble/TESTING_POLICY.md](research/lda_ensemble/TESTING_POLICY.md).
Its historical paths are relative to that consolidated directory. New model
work follows the same saved folds, fitting boundaries and selection rules.

Read [RESEARCH_HANDOFF.md](RESEARCH_HANDOFF.md) for current model provenance and
[AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for the experiment commit and submission tag convention.
The later [retention policy](research/lda_ensemble/EVIDENCE.html) supersedes
historical requirements to permanently retain unsuccessful per-fold outputs.
