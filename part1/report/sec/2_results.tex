\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Table~\ref{tab:p1first} is the first model family. The mixture by itself is the
weakest result. One logistic model over all five reasons beats a separate
classifier for each reason. Letting the model teach itself is the largest single
gain. Giving the mixture a small role in that step gives the best
cross-validated score in this family. That model, refit on all 235 real labels,
is the reported 0.89423 Kaggle submission. As noted above, its preprocessing saw
the test rows, so we do not treat it as the same experiment as the later models.

Table~\ref{tab:p1features} adds one extra input block at a time to that same
model. None of them raises validation sample-wise F1. Morgan fingerprints move
the cross-validated score by 0.004, which is inside the noise between repeats.
Later checks that added diseases, pathways, or eligibility text to an
embeddings-only logistic model also failed to help, and the pathway block
lowered the score. We kept the embeddings alone.

Table~\ref{tab:p1final} compares the later models on one shared set of checks.
The final model is the one described in Section~\ref{sec:methods}. On
cross-validation it is tied with the linear-discriminant ensemble: 0.9321
against 0.9318 on the 157 training labels, and 0.9214 against 0.9216 on all
235. Its 78-trial validation score is lower, 0.891 against 0.906. Its
time-ordered score is higher, 0.884 against 0.878. The reported Kaggle score
is 0.89636, above both 0.89423 and the ensemble's 0.89401.
We submitted it because that was the best reported test score, and the repeated
checks did not show a real loss.

On the 78 validation trials, the final blend scores 0.941 on Safety (9
trials), 0.667 on Funding (4), 0.759 on Efficacy (14), 0.990 on Recruitment
(50), and 0.471 on Other (10). The sample-wise average is 0.891 and the
macro average is 0.765. The two common reasons are handled well. Funding and
Other are not, and each rests on too few trials to trust a small difference.
The grouped cross-validation macro F1 is 0.883 on the 157 training labels and
0.850 on all 235.
