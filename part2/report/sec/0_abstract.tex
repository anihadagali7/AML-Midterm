\begin{abstract}
We predict whether a clinical trial meets its primary endpoint, separately
for Phase~I, II, and III. The score is log loss, so a confident wrong
probability is punished more than a near miss. The test trials start later
than the training trials, and later trials succeed more often.

We started from eligibility text and logistic regression, then compared
trees, drug features, and phase-specific fits. The model we kept is a stack
of text and drug classifiers, with more weight on recent trials, blended with
a neighbor model that also prefers recent similar trials. Our earlier
text-and-tree ensemble still made different mistakes, so the submission is
70\% of that recent-neighbor stack and 30\% of the earlier ensemble. The
reported Kaggle log loss is 0.58465. A later blend looked better on our
historical checks and scored 0.58492, so we kept the first of those two.
\end{abstract}
