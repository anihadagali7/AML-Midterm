The current model uses the original 150 embedding features. DBSCAN uses `eps=0.00012142366141394104` and `min_samples=5`; logistic regression uses `C=1000`, `solver="lbfgs"`, `tol=1e-4`, and `max_iter=5000`. Preserve that stopping tolerance: the penalty comparison below shows materially different validation predictions when the same L2 objective is optimized more tightly. Generated labels are accepted at estimated probability >=0.60 for positives and <=0.10 for negatives, with at least three known supporting trials, and only when both generated classes exist for a reason. Original labels keep raw weight 1; fitting weights are normalized to total 157 per reason.

Only **Efficacy** receives generated labels in this configuration: 8 positives and 125 negatives. The other four classifiers use the original 157 labeled trials. The classifier scaler is frozen from those 157 trials; the clustering scaler uses all 1,171 training trials. Validation is used for model selection, never for fitting.

Final predictions select every reason with probability >=0.50. If none qualifies, they select the single highest-probability reason. Keep this policy for the next feature comparisons.

### Reproduce or extend

Executed notebooks are saved in `part1/`: `additional_methods.ipynb`, `dbscan_refinement.ipynb`, `dbscan_label_tuning.ipynb`, `dbscan_weight_fine_tuning.ipynb`, and `dbscan_prediction_cutoffs.ipynb`. These new notebooks accept supplied CSVs either directly in `part1/` or in `part1/data/`. Each experiment folder preserves its settings, comparison tables, validation predictions, and relevant training datasets, label audits, and models.

`part1/classifier_features.ipynb` tests disease multi-hot indicators, broad pathway indicators with a missing-annotation flag, and eligibility-text TF-IDF independently, keeping DBSCAN targets and weights fixed. Training-only frequency profiles determine the tested filtering settings. Exact sparse classifier matrices, ordered feature names, shared targets/weights, preprocessing objects, models, and validation predictions are saved in `part1/experiments/classifier_features/`. No addition improves sample-wise F1; retain the embeddings-only reference. Feature combinations and new classifier regularization or feature-block weights have not been tested in this round.

`part1/classifier_feature_tuning.ipynb` follows up with C=1/10/100/1000 and added-block multipliers 0.25/1/4 for all three disease and all three criteria representations. It reuses the exact preprocessing, generated labels, and weights, with an embeddings-only control at each C. The 76 settings do not improve the current leader. Exact weighted matrices, models, predictions, and matched-control comparisons are preserved in `part1/experiments/classifier_feature_tuning/`. No pathway or combined-feature tuning is included.

`part1/classifier_combinations.ipynb` evaluates the four pairs/triple of diseases, criteria, and pathways with logistic regression at C=100/1000, plus embeddings-only controls. It freezes the existing diseases_min5, criteria_min3, and pathways_min30 representations and all pseudo-label settings. No combination beats the embeddings-only leader. Exact combined matrices and native preprocessing objects in `part1/experiments/classifier_combinations/` are available for a later nonlinear-model comparison; no nonlinear model is trained in this round.

`part1/nonlinear_combinations.ipynb` reuses those exact five input matrices for an RBF SVM grid: C=1/10/100 and gamma=0.001/scale/0.03. Each reason uses three-fold stratified, training-only sigmoid probability calibration, normalized fitting-weight total 157, cutoff 0.5, and the same fallback. All 45 models remain below the logistic leader. `part1/experiments/nonlinear_combinations/` preserves reused matrices, models, predictions, matched controls, calibration audits, and settings. The previous DBSCAN SVM control is reproduced, all saved models reload and rescore, and the selected SVM independently refits from saved inputs with matching probabilities.

`part1/remaining_classifier_features.ipynb` screens the other Part 1 columns independently at C=100/1000: actions, broad ICD categories, protein targets, raw drug names, matched drug names (minimum training counts 3/5), standardized year, and 19 molecular summary/coverage features from SMILES. Two annotation-missing controls and embeddings-only controls complete 30 configurations. Molecular descriptors use RDKit (installed in the workspace virtual environment; version recorded in settings). No addition beats the leader; matched names tie it, while year scores 0.826923 at C=100 and 0.750000 at C=1000. `part1/experiments/remaining_classifier_features/` saves exact matrices, fixed targets/weights, native preprocessing/models, predictions, frequency profiles, molecule parsing audits, and comparisons. No fingerprints or feature combinations are included in this screen.

`part1/gmm_pca.ipynb` isolates PCA before the existing tied-covariance K=10 GMM: no PCA versus 20/30/50 dimensions, fitted only on the 1,171 training trials without whitening. The classifier keeps all 150 embeddings and its frozen scaler, C=1000, label-generation rules, weight budgets, cutoff, and fallback. PCA30 is the best PCA version at sample F1 0.897436, below the original GMM at 0.905983 and DBSCAN at 0.910256. `part1/experiments/gmm_pca/` preserves exact clustering coordinates/responsibilities, generated datasets/weights, models, predictions, explained-variance tables, and replay checks. Cluster counts, covariance types, and self-training are unchanged in this comparison.

`part1/self_training.ipynb` starts from the fixed DBSCAN seed and refreshes additional labels only in originally seed-unknown training reason slots. Confidence 0.90/0.95, added-example caps 0.05/0.10, and one/two rounds give eight variants. Known and seed labels/weights are retained, and added class influence is controlled. The best sample-F1 variant is confidence 0.95, cap 0.10, two rounds: sample F1 0.901709, macro F1 0.812040. It improves Other F1 but lowers Efficacy/Recruitment F1 versus the seed. No validation/test rows enter self-training. Exact datasets, source/acceptance audits, teacher scores, models, and predictions are saved in `part1/experiments/self_training/`.

`part1/ani_ensemble.ipynb` reproduces Ani's validation-stage model with only 157 verified training labels and its original feature-only transductive preprocessing. A saved Ani source snapshot and fitted component make the comparison reproducible. The ensemble centers each model's log-odds at its native cutoff (ours 0.5, Ani 0.3), then mixes decision evidence at Ani weights 0/0.10/0.25/0.50/1. These are decision scores, not calibrated marginal probabilities. Both native endpoints are reproduced; no mix improves the current leader. `part1/experiments/ani_ensemble/` preserves components, policies, pseudo-label audits, and complementary errors: Ani is better on two trials, ours on five, and their sample-wise F1 is equal on 71. No final 235-label refit or Kaggle submission was performed.

`part1/gmm_pca_grid.ipynb` reuses the exact training-only PCA30 projection and compares K=10/20/30 with tied/diagonal covariance. The six settings keep all other GMM parameters, label-generation rules, weights, classifier features/settings, cutoff, and fallback fixed. The prior PCA30 tied/K10 control is reproduced and remains the best grid result at sample F1 0.897436; the K=30 diagonal setting scores 0.833333. Original no-PCA GMM (0.905983) and DBSCAN (0.910256) remain ahead. `part1/experiments/gmm_pca_grid/` preserves exact generated datasets, labeling/classifier models, coordinates/responsibilities, label audits, prediction sets, settings, and checksums. No additional trial features were added to clustering in this experiment.

`part1/clustering_feature_screen.ipynb` adds diseases_min5 (71 indicators) or criteria_min3 compressed to 20 training-only SVD dimensions to clustering, while the final classifier retains only the original embeddings. Added groups are centered, aggregate-RMS normalized, and scaled at 0.10/0.25/1. GMM retains K=10/tied covariance; DBSCAN retains min_samples=5 and recomputes radii at training-neighbor distance quantiles 0.25/0.50/0.75 for every representation. The 28-setting grid reproduces both original controls. GMM additions score lower; DBSCAN with either group at scale 0.25 and the median radius ties sample F1 and raises macro F1. Both change one incorrect Efficacy prediction to an incorrect Recruitment prediction. `part1/clustering_radius_controls.ipynb` checks embeddings alone at those exact radii: both score 0.893162, so radius changes alone do not reproduce the feature-model result. All 30 fits, exact inputs, generated datasets/weights, native models, predictions, and checks are preserved in `part1/experiments/clustering_feature_screen/`.

`part1/rare_label_weights.ipynb` crosses positive-class multipliers 1/1.5/2/3 for Funding and Other while keeping DBSCAN source labels/raw weights fixed. Only those two classifiers are refitted, with effective fitting weights renormalized to 157 per reason; C=1000, the embedding scaler, other estimators, cutoff, and fallback stay unchanged. All 16 configurations retain exactly the original validation predictions and F1. Parameter-effect checks confirm the weights alter coefficients/probabilities, but not selected labels. `part1/experiments/rare_label_weights/` saves fixed data, effective weight matrices, models, predictions, class-weight/precision/recall audits, and checksums. The existing current-model Kaggle CSV is verified to contain 312 nonempty predictions in sample-submission order. No final validation-label refit or Kaggle submission has been performed.

`part1/per_reason_regularization.ipynb` sweeps the user-selected C values 0.1/1/10/100/500/1000/2000 independently for each classifier, holding the other four at C=1000 and evaluating complete sample-wise F1. Thirty-five reason/C evaluations share one default control, giving 31 unique first-stage configurations. Each reason's strongest nondefault setting that ties/improves the baseline is shortlisted (C=500 for all five); all 26 subsets of two or more changes are then evaluated. None of the 57 unique configurations exceeds sample F1 0.910256. All-C=500 also ties the original, so the selected reference retains all-C=1000 under the declared tie preference. `part1/experiments/per_reason_regularization/` preserves all 35 binary fits, complete models/predictions, shared fixed data and normalized weights, individual sweeps, adaptive shortlist/combinations, checksums, and independent selected-model refit checks. No L1/Elastic Net or final validation-label refit is included in this experiment.

`part1/logistic_penalties.ipynb` crosses the same seven C values with L1 fractions 0/0.25/0.50/0.75/1, retaining fixed data, labels, weights, scaler, cutoff, and fallback. An initial iteration-limited SAGA attempt is archived under `part1/experiments/logistic_penalties/saga_probe/` and excluded. Final fits use full-batch SciPy L-BFGS-B on the equivalent convex logistic/Elastic Net objective, with nonnegative coefficient splitting for L1, an unpenalized intercept, KKT/gradient checks, and seven native scikit-learn objective cross-checks. All 175 binary fits converge; L1 and Elastic Net remain below the best matched converged L2 and the incumbent. A native same-solver diagnostic confirms the stopping-tolerance effect: LBFGS at C=1000 with tol=1e-9 scores 0.854701/0.703577, matching the full-batch C=1000 L2 labels, versus the saved tol=1e-4 model's 0.910256/0.811421. Earlier stopping may act as implicit regularization; that is an interpretation, not an established generalization gain. The original model/CSV remain unchanged. Exact coefficient fits, native diagnostics, data/weights, prediction sets, convergence audits, and source checksums are saved; coefficient-dictionary prediction is implemented in the notebook. No Kaggle submission or final validation-label refit was performed.

`part1/classifier_cross_validation.ipynb` uses five repeats of three-fold multilabel stratification (seeds 42–46; dependency `iterative-stratification==0.1.9`). Each fold rebuilds scalers, DBSCAN/GMM clustering, generated labels, weights, and classifiers after excluding held-out trials entirely from fitting. The same 15 splits compare supervised/DBSCAN/GMM baselines, DBSCAN tolerances 1e-3/3e-4/1e-4/3e-5/1e-5, standardized then unit-length embeddings at C=1/10/100/1000/10000/100000, and GMM probability blend weights 0/0.25/0.5/0.75/1. Sample F1 is pooled over the 157 held-out predictions per repeat before averaging repeats; variation and paired fold comparisons are descriptive. CV selections are frozen before the new settings are scored on supplied validation. `part1/experiments/classifier_cross_validation/` saves exact splits, fitting populations, generated datasets/raw and normalized weights, preprocessing/clustering models, all 256 model packets and prediction sets, fit/label audits, and independent disk scores. All three full-training baselines reproduce their saved targets/weights/probabilities, every model reloads, and 26 independent classifier refits reproduce probabilities. The current saved model and Kaggle file remain unchanged; no train+validation refit is performed.
